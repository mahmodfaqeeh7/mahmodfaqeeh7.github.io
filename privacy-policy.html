Glider Survival – Privacy Policy

Rawan Studio · Effective 16 November 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Glider Survival ("the game"), made by Rawan Studio ("we"), collects and why.

1. Information we collect ourselves

None. The game has no accounts, no sign-in and no analytics of our own. Your progress (scores, coins, unlocks, settings) is saved only on your device. We never receive it. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob: optional rewarded ads (for example "watch an ad to continue") and occasional full-screen ads between runs, never during play. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers,
  • approximate location derived from your IP address,
  • device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version),
  • ad interactions (ads shown, tapped or completed) and diagnostics (crash and performance data of the ads SDK).

Google uses this data as described in its own policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland): the game shows Google's consent form before personalised ads. You can change your choice any time in the game: Settings › Privacy choices (ads).
  • Advertising ID: on Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings › Privacy › Ads, which also opts you out of personalised ads.
  • No ads needed to play: every ad is optional except the occasional ad between runs.

4. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

5. Security and data sharing

We do not sell or share any data ourselves. Data collected by the AdMob SDK is sent to Google over encrypted connections.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
